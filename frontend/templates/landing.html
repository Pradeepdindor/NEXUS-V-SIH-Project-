System Blueprints

The 5-Layer Cognitive Brain

Distributed cyber-physical intelligence spanning edge vehicle reflexes to central spatial reasoning.

TIER 1

Sensory Ingestion

Dual 1080p cameras (Front/Cabin) synchronised with NMEA GPS at 10Hz, collecting real-world pavement footage.

TIER 2

Reflex Edge AI

CLAHE luminance enhancement, specular puddle glare suppression, and YOLOv8 multi-class defect classification.

TIER 3

Synaptic Cellular

Debounced event serialization with store-and-forward queue ensuring <1.5 MB/hour cellular data limits.

TIER 4

Cerebral Spatial Fusion

PostGIS Haversine clustering merges duplicate reports from multiple buses traversing the same road corridor.

TIER 5

Executive Dispatch

Automated SLA work-orders dispatched instantly to PWD, Traffic Police, and EMS 108 with GPS coordinates.
